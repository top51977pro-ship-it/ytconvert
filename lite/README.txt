YTConvert Setup
===============

Double-click "YTConvert Setup" in this folder to install YTConvert.

(If you opened the zip without extracting it, Windows asks to "Extract all"
first - click it, then open "YTConvert Setup" in the extracted folder.)

It installs just for you, with no admin rights, and downloads the video
engine (FFmpeg) during setup. Uninstall any time from Windows Settings >
Apps. Your downloaded files are always kept.

YTConvert: paste a YouTube link, pick the quality, get the original file -
MP4 up to 4K / 60 fps or MP3 up to 320 kbps - or cut out just the part you
need. Everything runs on your own computer.

Only download videos you own or have permission to download.
